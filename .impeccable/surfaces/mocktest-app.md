# MockTest workspace surface brief

Mode: Operate

## Pinned direction

Clear exam desk — focused and quiet, with strong progress cues. This direction was selected by the user and governs the workspace screens.

## World

Treat the interface as a clean sheet prepared for a real practice session: readable paper-like work areas, clear ink-like hierarchy, restrained accents, and generous room around question text. Do not decorate with classroom clip art or fake exam stationery.

## First viewport

On the dashboard, show the next useful action, saved test names, durations, question counts, and the latest completed scores. Make importing a new test easy to find. On an exam screen, the question and choices lead; the deadline and progress remain visible without competing with them.

## Visitor path

Bring JSON → inspect validation feedback → save the test → start or resume an attempt → answer and navigate → submit → review the result → return to history or retake.

## Signature interaction

Direct question navigation shows answered and unanswered state. The countdown is visible throughout the exam, and saved answers survive refresh.

## Cross-surface reach

Use one restrained set of text, spacing, color, control, and status styles across import, dashboard, exam, result, and history. Keep the exam route visually quieter and free of workspace navigation.

## Honest risk

This is a first-party workflow concept; the user selected its quiet, task-first character. Keep the product legible and familiar for students who are concentrating under time pressure.

## Scope guardrails

Preserve the SRS requirements, exact JSON v1 rules, Google/GitHub-only Clerk sign-in, server-side ownership and scoring, the 1 MB import limit, and support from 360 px upward. Do not invent AI generation, student outcomes, customer proof, or teacher workflows.
